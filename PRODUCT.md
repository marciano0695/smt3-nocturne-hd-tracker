# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

One player running a single playthrough of Shin Megami Tensei III: Nocturne HD Remaster, who wants to see every ending from one save file and build a strength-focused Demi-fiend. They use the tracker mid-session, on a phone beside the TV and on a laptop or second monitor in equal measure: quick glances at what to do next, logging a level-up the moment it happens, and longer planning sessions for the build and the story order.

## Product Purpose

Nocturne punishes mistakes that can't be undone: one skill per level-up, eight skill slots, declined skills gone for good, and endings locked behind save files that must never be overwritten. The tracker turns those systems into clear next steps: where to hard save, whether to do story, Labyrinth of Amala or side content next, which Magatama to equip at each upcoming level-up, and how close the final 8-skill build is. Success is reaching every ending from one save with the target build and no wasted level-up or overwritten hard save.

## Positioning

The one place that knows the player's exact save and tells them the next right move, so nothing irreversible happens by accident.

## Brand Personality

Nocturne-flavoured: it echoes the game's mood (quiet, eerie, Tokyo after the Conception) without copying its menus, and stays a clear tool first. The voice is calm and precise. Warnings about irreversible choices are direct, never alarmist. Three words: quiet, eerie, precise.

## Anti-references

- A SaaS admin template: stat cards, gradients, big hero numbers, the usual dashboard kit.
- A cluttered fan wiki: walls of tables, no hierarchy, everything shouting at once.
- Gamer RGB or neon: glowing neon accents, angular "gamer" chrome.
- Cute or bubbly: pastels, pill-shaped everything, playful mascot energy.
- A copy of the game's own menus.

## Design Principles

- **Irreversible moments are loud; everything else is quiet.** Hard saves, declining a skill and overwriting a slot get unmistakable treatment, so routine data can stay calm.
- **Next move first.** Every screen leads with the one thing to do now, with the reasoning one step away.
- **Honest about uncertainty.** Unverified assumptions stay tagged until the player confirms them, and nothing about the game is invented.
- **Mood through restraint.** The atmosphere comes from tone, type and light, never from decoration that slows reading.
- **The save is sacred.** Player data is never lost; destructive actions are confirmed or undoable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No specific requirements from the player.
